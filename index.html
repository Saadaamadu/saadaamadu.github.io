I work across research, policy, and program design on food systems and climate resilience, with a particular focus on Sub-Saharan Africa. I'm currently a PhD student in City and Regional Planning at UC Berkeley, where my research examines food security, underutilized crops, and economic development — recent work looks at foreign exchange shortages and food security in landlocked Malawi, and the political economy of fonio as it moves from a "poverty food" toward global superfood status.

Before returning to graduate school, I spent close to a decade in practice: most recently as Program Manager for Climate Resilience & Equity at Resilient Cities Network, where I led survey and scorecard work across a global network of cities, and previously as a Policy Researcher at Rethink Food and a Sustainability Analyst at Schneider Electric. I hold an M.S. in Environmental Sciences & Policy from Central European University and a B.A. in International Studies from the College of Staten Island.

I speak Dagbanli and English natively, along with Twi and Hausa. Feel free to reach out — see the contact page for details.
