I am a PhD student in City and Regional Planning at UC Berkeley, concentrating in Food Systems and Applied Data Science. My research looks at food security, underutilized crops, and economic development in Sub-Saharan Africa — recent work examines foreign exchange shortages and food security in landlocked Malawi, and the political economy of fonio as it moves from a "poverty food" toward global superfood status.

Before starting my doctoral work, I spent close to a decade working across food systems, climate resilience, and sustainability — most recently as Program Manager for Climate Resilience & Equity at Resilient Cities Network, and previously as a Policy Researcher at Rethink Food and a Sustainability Analyst at Schneider Electric. I hold an M.S. in Environmental Sciences & Policy from Central European University and a B.A. in International Studies from the College of Staten Island.

I speak Dagbanli and English natively, along with Twi and Hausa. Feel free to reach out — see the contact page for details.
